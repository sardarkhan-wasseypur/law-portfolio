Appellate Jurisprudence & Practice

Biography & Advocacy Philosophy

Advocate Shubham Singh commenced his litigation practice across the trial divisions of Delhi, grounding his courtroom acumen in rigorous cross-examination, urgent interlocutory practice, and procedural jurisprudence under the Civil Procedure Code and Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (erstwhile CrPC).

Over the preceding decade, his chambers expanded extensively into substantive constitutional writ jurisprudence, commercial disputes, white-collar defense, and Special Leave Petitions before the Supreme Court of India and the Delhi High Court. His advocacy combines deep constitutional doctrine with meticulous evidentiary dossiers.

The practice prioritizes early statutory defense, preemptive protection against arbitrariness in administrative state actions, and rigorous oral advocacy. Every brief admitted into chambers is subjected to rigorous mooting, comprehensive precedent cross-referencing, and structural statutory scrutiny.

"The credibility of an advocate is forged not in rhetorical flourish, but in unyielding factual fidelity and mastery of statutory architecture."
