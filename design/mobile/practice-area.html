Inquiries & Answers

Frequently Addressed Questions

Can Anticipatory Bail be moved directly before Delhi High Court? expand_more
While concurrent jurisdiction exists under Section 438 CrPC, the established convention requires filing before the Sessions Court first, unless extraordinary circumstances (e.g., immediate apprehension of arrest during court holidays, multi-state jurisdictional conflicts, or high-profile agency action) warrant a direct petition.
What is the procedure during court vacations? expand_more
The Delhi High Court designates Vacation Benches for urgent matters concerning imminent arrest and personal liberty. A formal mentioning slip outlining urgency must be submitted to the designated Vacation Registrar before 11:00 AM for same-day or next-day listing.
Required documents for S. 482 Quashing Petitions? expand_more
Certified true copy of the FIR, Charge-Sheet (if filed), witness statements under Section 161 CrPC, original civil agreements or contractual paper trails showing non-criminal dispute nature, and supporting case citations showing patent abuse of authority.
