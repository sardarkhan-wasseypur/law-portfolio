Chamber Procedure

Appellate Case Pathway

A disciplined 3-step institutional methodology ensuring rigorous brief preparation before judicial entry.

1

Confidential Docket Review

Step 01

Litigants share certified trial court record, charge-sheets, or impugned appellate orders under strict attorney-client privilege. Initial assessment of constitutional maintainability.

2

Senior Merits Conference

Step 02

Comprehensive 45-minute in-person or encrypted video conference at the Delhi Chambers. Distillation of legal propositions, precedents, and bench precedents.

3

Drafting & High Court Listing

Step 03

Pleadings vetted and filed through the Supreme Court/Delhi High Court e-filing portal. Urgent mentioning for supplementary listing before the Hon'ble Chief Justice's bench.
